Good questions

A few
honest answers.

Will it work with my Bluetooth headphones?+

Yes. The first time you use a new pair, the app plays a quick sound and listens for it to come back. It measures the delay, then remembers that pair for next time. Wired headphones work too.

Can I hear my own vocals in my headphones?+

You won’t hear live monitoring of your own voice. Once the layers build up, slip your headphones off one ear. One ear on the mix, one on the room, just like tracking in a vocal booth.

Does it replace my DAW?+

It’s the step before. Catch and shape the song while it’s still alive, then export your mixed master or stems into Ableton, or any DAW, when you’re ready for full production. You can also bring a rough mix or stems into Songwriting to work out your next part.

Do you keep or sell my recordings?+

No. They stay on your phone unless you turn on your own iCloud backup, which is off by default. We never collect your data, so there’s nothing to sell.
